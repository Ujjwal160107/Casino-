# Privacy Policy for Fortuna Bot

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

Fortuna Bot ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Discord bot and associated services. By adding Fortuna Bot to your server or using its commands, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use specific commands or features:
*   **User IDs & Usernames:** We store your Discord User ID and Username to create and maintain your unique economy profile.
*   **Profile Data:** Information generated through gameplay, including wallet balances, bank balances, inventory items, job status, marriage status, and game history.
*   **Server Configuration:** If you are a server administrator, we store configuration settings for your guild (e.g., custom currency, tax rates, shop items).

### B. Information Automatically Collected
*   **Guild IDs:** We store the unique ID of the Discord servers (Guilds) where the bot is installed to maintain separate economy environments for each server.
*   **Timestamps:** We record timestamps for cooldown tracking (e.g., daily claim times, work shift completion) and account creation dates.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the specific purpose of providing the bot’s services:
*   **Core Functionality:** To process transactions, calculate game outcomes, and manage your virtual inventory and career.
*   **Persistence:** To ensure your progress is saved and available when you return to the bot.
*   **Permissions:** To verify if you have the necessary administrative rights to execute specific commands (e.g., `!setup`, `!add-money`).
*   **Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns and improve bot performance and features.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We are committed to protecting your data:
*   **No Third-Party Sales:** We do **NOT** sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.
*   **No Advertising:** We do not use your data for third-party advertising or tracking purposes.
*   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

## 4. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. Your data is stored in a secure MongoDB database with restricted access. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.

## 5. Your Data Rights

You have control over your data:
*   **Access:** You can view all data stored about you by using the `!profile`, `!balance`, and `!inventory` commands.
*   **Deletion:**
    *   **User Data:** You may request the deletion of your account data by contacting the bot developers in our Support Server.
    *   **Guild Data:** Server Administrators can delete all data associated with their server using the `!reset-economy` command.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will verify and delete such information from our records.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
